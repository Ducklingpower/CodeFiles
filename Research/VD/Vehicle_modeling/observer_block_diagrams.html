Vehicle model: bicycle and dual track

Block diagrams and the math of vehicle_model.m, the MATLAB reference for the on-vehicle vehicle_model package: road wheel angles, kinematic slips, normal load observer, forces, the two MPC brush vy observers, the per tire Fy split and the understeer gradient. Every numbered block points at the line that computes it. The build plan for the C++ is at the end.

0 · What the package computes

Four stages. The kinematics give the reference slips and the slip ratios. The load model and the strain gages give the observed normal load. The force balance and the wheel dynamics give the forces. The slip observers turn the axle force and the observed load into slip angles without the localization vy, and those give the understeer gradient.

Every filter is a first order Tustin low pass with prewarp (lpf, vehicle_model.m:914): K = tan(π fc Ts), y(k) = (1−K)/(1+K)·y(k−1) + K/(1+K)·(x(k) + x(k−1)), Ts = 0.01 s. Derivatives are backward differences of filtered signals (ddt, :262).

1 · Road wheel angles and kinematic slips

Every tire uses its own road wheel angle. The steering wheel angle goes through the steering ratio and offset, then the Ackermann table gives the left and right front angle, and the static toe is added on top (0 until it is confirmed on the car). The bicycle model uses the mean of the two front angles.

2 · Normal load model and observer

The load model turns the vehicle motion into a load per tire (Fig. 1a). The observer adds the part of the strain gage change the model misses (Fig. 1b): the model sets the level, the gages add their rate.

4 · Bicycle vy observer (MPC brush at the observed axle load)

The MPC brush tire inverts the axle force at the observed axle load into an axle slip. Kinematics turn the two slips into a vy, the IMU integrates vy between corrections. The localization vy is not an input. Full trust: the tire model vy is the mean of the two axles.

5 · Per tire Fy, dual track vy observer and kus

The axle force is split per tire twice: by normal load, and by normal load corrected with each tire's brush at its bicycle observer slip (Jung & Choi). The corrected split is the per tire Fy output and the input of the dual track observer. The understeer gradient uses the controller's formula with a secant axle stiffness; the right column shows where the bicycle version differs.

The bicycle kus is the one to use; the dual track one still reads high (front load transfer).

7 · Plan: building the dual track model in on-vehicle vehicle_model

Today the on-vehicle dual track node (src/control/vehicle_model, dynamic_dualtrack_model) publishes kinematic slip angles and slip ratios from the odom at 3 Hz with one front road wheel angle, and the bicycle node has an axle load model and observer. The plan below brings the node to exactly what vehicle_model.m does, one tested piece at a time, with the MATLAB script as the golden reference.

Phase 1

Golden data from MATLAB

  1. Add an export to vehicle_model.m: inputs at 100 Hz exactly as the node receives them (odom vx, vy, r, quaternion, accel_filtered ax, ay, steering angle, wheel speeds, gages, brake pressures, rpm, throttle, gear) and every output of section 6, as CSV.
  2. Cut two short windows (a few laps of the 2026-09-03 log and one braking / lock-up window) for unit tests.
Phase 2

Pure C++ blocks, no ROS, each with a unit test against the golden CSV

  1. Filters: a Tustin prewarped first order low pass to match lpf (the node's current lowPassFilter is the exponential form, slightly different); 3 Hz shared, 0.3 Hz load model inputs, 0.5 Hz gages and the model compare copy.
  2. Road wheel angles: load the Ackermann table as a CSV parameter, linear interpolation, toe parameters; δi for all four tires and the bicycle δ.
  3. Kinematics: one tire_kin(vx, vy, r, δ[4]) used for the reference slips, the slip ratios and both observers; replace the single steering angle in calculate_slip_angles and slip_ratio_calc with δi.
  4. Load model: the bicycle loads with a_z = g·cosθ·cosφ − v_x·r·sinφ (the C++ bicycle model has the bank term commented out), downforce, the dual track lateral transfer and the corner split.
  5. Fz observer: per tire, the model passed through the gage filter before the rate compare; c(k) = a·(c(k−1) + K·u). The existing vehicle_model::Observer computes c = (1−leak)·c + K·e, which matches with leak = 1 − τ/(τ + dt) = 0.00125 and K_c++ = K·τ/(τ + dt) = 1.0986 at τ 8 s, K 1.1, dt 0.01 s.
  6. Forces: axle Fy force and yaw moment balance with the front in its tire frame; Fx total; Fx per tire from the wheel dynamics (engine map CSV, gear and diff ratios and efficiencies, brake gain, inertias, locked cap).
  7. Brush: forward, closed form inverse and conditioning as one small header shared with fbl_mpc_controller (the closed form equals its cubic).
  8. Bicycle vy observer, Fy split + correction, dual track vy observer, kus, in that order, each tested on the golden CSV (tolerance: vy 1e-3 m/s, slips 1e-3 deg, kus 1e-6).
Phase 3

Node, parameters, message

  1. Subscriptions: /odometry/global_filtered, /novatel_bottom/accel_filtered, steering_extended_report, wheel_speed_report, tire_report, brake_2_report, pt_report; one 100 Hz update.
  2. Parameters from vehicle_model.m: lf 1.724 / lr 1.248 m (URDF base_link), tracks, m, h, Iz, aero, roll centres and wheel rates, steering ratio and offset, Ackermann table, toe, brush μ and Cα, observer τ, K, gains and speed gates.
  3. Message: extend DualTrackSlip (or a new DualTrackState) with every output of section 6 and valid flags (observer running, tire unloaded, wheel locked).
Phase 4

Validation

  1. Bag replay of the 2026-09-03 log through the node, outputs against vehicle_model.m on the whole log.
  2. A second log (2025 comp) to check that nothing was tuned to one session.
  3. On the car in shadow mode: published, not used by the controller.
Phase 5

Use in the MPC

  1. fbl_mpc_controller: take the bicycle kus (or the observed axle Fz and slip into its own effective_stiffness) in place of the fixed load brush; replace the 0.0012 clamp with a hold or rate limit when |F|/μFz > ~0.9.
Open items

To settle on the car or in the data

  • Static toe: yaw_moment.m has −0.451° front and rear, set to 0 here until confirmed.
  • Wheel speed calibration: the 2026 log has too few free rolling samples (289 < 300), wheelCal = 1.
  • Iz (placeholder 1000 kg m²), CG height, front ARB rate.
  • The dual track kus reads high against the measured one; the bicycle kus is the one for the MPC.
